[h1]Game Consultant[/h1]
An in-game advisor that plans your games with the game's own rules. No more alt-tabbing to wiki tables: every choice you make while creating a game is rated on the spot, and one click builds the perfect combo for you.

It only uses what [b]your[/b] company has: the topics you researched, the features you unlocked (multi-genre, target audience, multi-platform), the consoles on the market [i]right now[/i], your licences, your cash, your staff, your game history and the current trend.

[h2]Game Concept[/h2]
[list]
[*]Every topic, genre, second genre, platform and audience button gets a badge: [b]★ Best[/b], or how close the best game you can still make with it gets to your best option (e.g. 92%), plus the direct match (+++ RPG, Genre ++ · Audience +++).
[*]Weak options are dimmed, or hidden and sorted best-first with [b]Hide poor options[/b].
[*]A Consultant card rates your combo (topic/genre, topic/audience, platform/genre, platform/audience, trend, multi-platform tech gap) and warns you about repeating your last game.
[*][b]Create perfect combo automatically[/b] picks topic, genre(s), audience and platforms in one click. It keeps the game size and platform slots you chose, only uses consoles you own (and tells you when a licence would be worth it), and prefers combos you haven't made yet.
[/list]

[h2]Development stages[/h2]
[list]
[*]Each slider shows how much that area matters for [i]this[/i] game (genre and topic, including the topic overrides the wiki tables miss), a recommended value and a marker.
[*]The plan covers all three stages: it chases the review bonuses for focusing on the right areas, avoids the penalties for neglecting them, and steers your tech/design ratio to the genre target.
[*][b]Apply recommendation[/b] sets the sliders, or let [b]Auto-apply[/b] do it every stage.
[/list]

[h2]Employees (medium games and up)[/h2]
[list]
[*]Suggests who should be responsible for each area, using their design/tech skills, specialisations and workload, and [b]Apply sliders + staff[/b] assigns them for you.
[*]Warns about unassigned areas and overloaded staff, and counts the experts you need for a perfect score (and for AAA).
[*]The concept card compares your team with the ideal size for the game, and for AAA the perfect combo favours genres your experts cover.
[/list]

[h2]Sequels and expansion packs[/h2]
[list]
[*]When you pick a game for a sequel, a window tells you if it is a good idea right now, e.g. [i]"This game is currently a great pick for a sequel on Playsystem 2 for Everyone audiences."[/i]
[*]It checks the 40-week rule (counting your real development time), whether you have a newer engine than the original (+0.2 and more hype), retired consoles and repeat penalties, and ranks your best sequel candidates.
[/list]

[h2]Publishing deals[/h2]
[list]
[*]Ranks the offers by how close a game each deal still lets you make to your best one, checks the minimum review score against what you can expect, and shows pay, royalties, licence costs and deals that need research first.
[/list]

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*]Made for Game Dev Tycoon 1.7.x (tested on 1.7.9). Needs the official Game Dev Tycoon Mod API, which ships with the game.
[*]It reads the game's own data and formulas, so modded topics and consoles are rated too.
[*]It never changes your save: it only fills in the same fields you would. You can disable it at any time.
[*]Settings (hide poor options, auto-apply) are remembered with the game's settings.
[/list]
