[h1]Game Consultant[/h1]
An in-game advisor built on the game's own rules. It rates your choices as you plan a game and can pick the perfect combo in one click. It only uses what your company has: researched topics, unlocked features, consoles on the market now, your staff and your history.

[h2]Game Concept[/h2]
[list]
[*]Topics, genres, audiences and platforms get a badge: [b]★ Best[/b] or a score like 92%.
[*]Poor options are dimmed, or hidden with [b]Hide poor options[/b].
[*]A side card rates your combo and warns about repeats.
[*][b]Create perfect combo automatically[/b] fills everything in.
[/list]

[h2]Development stages[/h2]
[list]
[*]Recommended slider values for every stage, based on your exact topic and genre.
[*]From medium games on, suggests who to put on each area based on skills, experts and workload.
[*][b]Apply[/b] sets it all, or turn on [b]Auto-apply[/b].
[*]Flags extra features that would be [b]wasted[/b] at your sliders, so you stop paying for them.
[/list]

[h2]Sequels, deals and consoles[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Sequels:[/b] tells you which games are a great pick right now, and when to wait.
[*][b]Publishing deals:[/b] ranks offers by how good a game they let you make.
[*][b]Your own consoles:[/b] counts the extra consoles a hit sells.
[/list]

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*]For Game Dev Tycoon 1.7.x. Needs the Mod API that ships with the game.
[*]Works with modded topics and consoles.
[*]Safe to turn off at any time.
[/list]
